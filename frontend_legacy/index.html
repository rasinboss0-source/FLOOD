AI Flash Flood Prediction Model
🧠 Model Architecture & Methodology

The model utilizes a Calibrated Random Forest Ensemble trained on historical flood events across India. It integrates 20 hydrologically engineered features into a single continuous probability score (0% to 100%) for the critical 1–3 hour flash flood lead window.

Unlike standard weather reports that report only rainfall volume, this system accounts for how rainfall interacts with local terrain (upstream catchment size, DEM slope, clay saturation, and river surge velocity).

Hydrological Physics: The Rational Method
Peak Discharge: Q = C × I × A Where:
  • C (Runoff Coefficient): Dependent on soil clay % and moisture saturation.
  • I (Rainfall Intensity): 1-hour cloudburst rate in mm/hr.
  • A (Catchment Area): Total upstream drainage basin in km².
